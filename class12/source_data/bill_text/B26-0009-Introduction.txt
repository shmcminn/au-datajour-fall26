A BILL 
 
_________________________ 
 
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA 
 
_________________________ 
 
 
To amend the Automobile Consumer Protection Act of 1984 to remove exemptions for 1 
motorcycles and motor-driven cycles, and to require a motor vehicle dealer to provide 2 
written notice of a vehicle’s classification and registration requirements to consumers 3 
before selling a vehicle; to amend Section 4 of the District of Columbia Revenue Act of 4 
1937 concerning registration of motor vehicles, to add as unlawful acts the rental of any 5 
motorcycle or motor-driven cycle affixed with false identifying information of vehicle 6 
class or that has not been properly registered; and to amend the Rental Vehicle Tax 7 
Reform Act of 1978 to remove the exemption of motorcycles and motor-driven cycles 8 
from rental fleet registration requirements.  9 
 10 
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 11 
act may be cited as the “Moped Registration Accountability Amendment Act of 2025”. 12 
 Sec. 2. The Automobile Consumer Protection Act of 1984, effective March 14, 1985 13 
(D.C. Law 5-162, D.C. Official Code § 50-501 et seq) is amended as follows:  14 
 (a) Section 2(9) (D.C. Official Code § 50-501(9)) is amended to read as follows:  15 
 “(9) “Motor vehicle” means a motor vehicle, including motorcycles and motor-driven 16 
cycles, manufactured for sale, offered for sale, sold, or registered in the District and which is 17 
designed for the primary purpose of transporting a driver and 1 or more passengers on streets, 18 
roads, or highways. The term “motor vehicle” shall not include buses sold for public 19 
transportation, motor homes, or motorized recreational vehicles.”. 20

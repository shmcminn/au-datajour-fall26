To amend the District of Columbia Workers’ Compensation Act of 1979 to provide that the 15 
payment or award of compensation under the workers’ compensation law of any other 16 
state shall not bar a claim for compensation under the District’s workers’ compensation 17 
law for the same injury or death; provided, that any such award under the District’s 18 
workers’ compensation law shall be reduced by the amount of compensation received or 19 
awarded under the workers’ compensation law of any other state. 20 
  21 
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 22 
act may be cited as the “Parity in Workers’ Compensation Recovery Act of 2025”. 23 
 Sec. 2. Section 4(a-1) of the District of Columbia Workers’ Compensation Act of 1979, 24 
effective July 1, 1980 (D.C. Law 3-77; D.C. Official Code § 32-1503(a-1)), is amended to read 25 
as follows:  26 
“(a-1)(1) Notwithstanding any other law, the payment or award of compensation under 27 
the workers’ compensation law of any other state to an employee or his dependent shall not bar a 28 
claim for compensation under this act for the same injury or death; provided, that any such award 29 
of compensation under this act shall be reduced by the amount of compensation received or 30 
awarded under the workers’ compensation law of any other state. 31 
“(2) This subsection shall apply to all claims under this act that are pending as of 32 
the effective date of the Parity in Workers’ Compensation Recovery Emergency Amendment Act 33

2022, as introduced on June 6, 2022 (Bill 24-841).”. 36

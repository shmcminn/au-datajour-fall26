To amend the Department of Youth Rehabilitation Services Establishment Act of 2004 to require 25 
the Mayor to publish within 120 days a plan to address persistent overcrowding of the 26 
Youth Services Center and to require the Department of Youth Rehabilitation Services to 27 
publish publicly certain population data pertaining to youth at Youth Services Center and 28 
New Beginnings Youth Development Center. 29 
 30 
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 31 
act may be cited as the “Strengthening Capacity and Transparency at DYRS Amendment Act of 32 
2025”. 33 
Sec. 2. The Department of Youth Rehabilitation Services Establishment Act of 2004, 34 
effective April 12, 2005 (D.C. Law 15-335; D.C. Official Code § 2-1515.01 et seq.), is amended 35 
as follows: 36 
(a) Section 104b (D.C. Official Code § 2–1515.04b) is amended by adding new 37

2 
subsections (c-d) to read as follows:   38 
“(c) Notwithstanding juvenile confidentiality laws pursuant to D.C. Official Code § 2–39 
1515.06, the Department shall publicly report on their website population data for youth at the 40 
Youth Services Center and New Beginnings Youth Development Center. The reported 41 
information shall include:  42 
“(1) Daily population counts by status at entry, race, and age; 43 
“(2) Average length of stay for all youth within the facility; and   44 
“(3) The monthly number of youth who: 45 
“(A) Are newly admitted to the YSC already with committed status; or  46 
“(B) Convert to committed status from another legal status while at YSC 47 
“(C) Are placed in instances of improper room confinement pursuant to § 48 
24–912, including instances in which they are confined to their room for a period exceeding 6 49 
hours.”  50 
“(4) The average number of days confined for committed youth at release from 51 
the YSC, by month of admission or conversion to committed status; and  52 
“(5) Monthly critical incident and assault data by incident type, facility, and 53 
month.” 54 
“(6) Recidivism data which shall be measured using the following metrics:  55 
“(A) The percentage of youth who completed rehabilitation under 56 
commitment to DYRS for which the Office of the Attorney General has, within three years of 57 
release from DYRS commitment, opened a new delinquency matter unrelated to the offense for 58 
which the youth was placed under commitment to DYRS. 59

“(B) The percentage of youth who completed rehabilitation under 60 
commitment to DYRS for which the Office of the Attorney General has, within three years of 61 
release from DYRS commitment, sought prosecutorial action in relation to a juvenile’s alleged 62 
criminal behavior unrelated to the offense for which the youth was placed under commitment to 63 
DYRS. 64 
“(d) Within 120 days after the effective date of Strengthening Capacity and Transparency 65 
of DYRS Amendment Act of 2025, the Mayor shall submit to the Council a one-year action plan 66 
to address overcrowding and the awaiting placement population at the Youth Services Center.  67 
The plan shall specify how the Department will permanently reduce the juvenile population at 68 
the Youth Services Center to a maximum of 90 youth and avoid future instances of 69 
overcrowding. 70

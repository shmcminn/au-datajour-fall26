To amend the Historic Landmark and Historic District Protection Act of 1978 to require the 16 
Historic Preservation Review Board to include Historic African American burial grounds 17 
in its inventory of historic landmarks and historic districts, and on city maps, and to 18 
require the Mayor to include the preservation status of Historic African American burial 19 
grounds in existing annual reporting requirements to the Council. 20 
 21 
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 22 
act may be cited as the “Inventory of Historic African American Burial Grounds Amendment 23 
Act of 2025”.  24 
 Sec. 2. The Historic Landmark and Historic District Protection Act of 1978, effective 25 
March 3, 1979 (D.C. Law 2-144; D.C. Code § 6-1101 et seq.), is amended as follows:   26 
 (a) Section 3 (D.C. Official Code § 6-1102) is amended as follows:  27 
  (1) A new paragraph (4B) is added to read as follows:  28 
  “(4B) "Historic African American burial ground" means a cemetery or any natural 29 
or prepared physical location, whether originally below, on, or above the surface of the earth, 30 
into which human remains are deposited as a part of the death rite or ceremony of a culture, and 31 
that was established in the District of Columbia prior to January 1, 1955, for interments of 32 
African Americans and is owned by a public body or qualified charitable organization.”. 33

2 
 
 (b) Section 4 (D.C. Official Code § 6-1103(c)(3)) is amended to read as follows: 34 
  “(3) Designate and maintain a current inventory of historic landmarks and historic 35 
districts in the District of Columbia and, in connection therewith, adopt and publish appropriate 36 
procedures. In coordination with the Office of Planning, the inventory shall include: 37 
   (A) All identified Historic African American burial grounds within the 38 
District of Columbia including locational data, ownership records, and any contested issues 39 
related to burial grounds; and 40 
   (B) The representation of all identified Historic African American burial 41 
grounds on city maps including the District of Columbia Geographic Information System and 42 
other public planning documents; and”. 43 
 (c) Section 14 (D.C. Official Code § 6-1113) is amended as follows:  44 
  (1) Paragraph (2) is amended by striking the phrase “; and” and inserting a 45 
semicolon in its place.  46 
  (2) Paragraph (3) is amended by striking the phrase “building violations.” and 47 
inserting the phrase “building violations; and” in its place.  48 
  (3) A new paragraph (4) is added to read as follows:  49 
  “(4) The preservation status of Historic African American burial grounds, 50 
including efforts to register sites that are not yet listed in the District of Columbia’s inventory of 51 
historic sites or on the National Register of Historic Places.”.  52

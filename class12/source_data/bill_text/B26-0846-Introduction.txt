A BILL 
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA 
To authorize the Mayor to dispose of an approximately 15-acre parcel of real property located at 
Poplar Point. 
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 
act may be cited as the "Poplar Point Economic Development Anchor Disposition Act of 2026". 
Sec. 2. Definitions. 
For the purpose of this Act, the term: 
(1) "Act" means An Act Authorizing the sale of certain real estate in the District of 
Columbia no longer required for public purposes, approved August 5, 1939 (53 Stat. 1211; D.C. 
Official Code§ 10-801 et seq.). 
(2) "CBE Act" means the Small and Certified Business Enterprise Development and 
Assistance Act of 2005, effective October 20, 2005 (D.C. Law 16-33; D.C. Official Code§ 2-
218.01 et seq.). 
(3) "Certified Business Enterprise" means a business enterprise or joint venture certified 
pursuant to the Small and Certified Business Enterprise Development and Assistance Act of 
2005, effective October 20, 2005 (D.C. Law 16-33; D.C. Official Code§ 2-218.01 et seq.).

 Sec. 3.  Findings. 52 
 (a) The Property is owned by the United States and is under the jurisdiction of the 53 
National Park Service (“NPS”).  54

 (b) In 2006, Congress enacted the Federal and District of Columbia Government Real 55 
Property Act of 2006, approved December 15, 2006 (120 Stat. 2711) (“Federal Act”), directing 56 
the National Park Service to transfer Poplar Point to the District upon satisfaction by the District 57 
of certain requirements set forth in the Federal Act.  58 
 (c) On August 22, 2024, the District issued a Request for Expressions of Interest for a 59 
Destination Anchor at Poplar Point. 60 
 (d) Following a competitive and public solicitation process, the Mayor, through the 61 
Office of the Deputy Mayor for Planning and Economic Development, selected Developer’s 62 
proposal to develop approximately 15 acres at Poplar Point into a state-of-the-art health and 63

wellness facility and related amenities (the “Project”). 65

Transaction Documents.   68

TERM SHEET 
 
THERME-POPLAR POINT 
 
Date September 17, 2026 
Seller (Landlord) District of Columbia (the “ District”), acting by and through the 
Office of the Deputy Mayor for Planning and Economic 
Development (“DMPED”). 
Buyer (Tenant) Therme DC LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, and its 
successors, assigns, or affiliates (the “ Developer”), as approved by 
DMPED or permitted under the agr eements between District and 
Developer that memorialize the terms of the disposition of the 
Property, which shall be consistent with this Term Sheet (the 
“Transaction Documents”). 
Property An approximately fifteen (15) acre parcel located at Poplar Point as 
such parcel will be described in the Transaction Documents. 
Transaction 
Documents 
All of the terms and conditions of the lease and development of the 
Property will be governed by the terms of the Transaction 
Documents by and between the District and the Developer. 
Method of Disposition The Property will be conveyed by gr ound lease with a term of sixty 
(60) years with one (1) twenty (20) year extension option.  
Consideration The annual rent for the Property w ill be one dollar ($1.00) for the 
first twenty (20) years of the lease term (“ Initial Rent ”). At the 
beginning of the twentieth (20th) ye ar of the lease term, the Parties 
shall select an appraiser to determ ine the fair market value of the 
Property for purposes of calculating the ground rent to be payable by 
Developer commencing with the twenty-first (21st) year of the lease 
term (the “ Base Ground Rent ”). The Base Ground Rent will be 
recalculated at the beginning of each of the fiftieth (50th) and sixtieth 
(60th) years of the lease term by having the Property reappraised 
(each a “ Rent Reset ”). Commencing with the twenty-sixth (26 th) 
year of the lease term, the Ba se Ground Rent shall increase once 
every five (5) years thereafter, except in a year when a Rent Reset is 
occurring, by the then-current a nnual Consumer Price Index for the 
United States of America (the “ Five-Year Rent Adjustment ”). In 
all events the Five-Year Rent Adju stment shall increase the then 
current Base Ground Rent by a minimum of two percent (2.0%) per 
year, but not more than three pe rcent (3.0%) per year, with the 
applicable annual percen tage increases during such five (5) year 
period being cumulative but not compounded.   
The Project The Developer will construct a state-of-the-art health and well-being 
destination to include an approximately 400,000-600,000 square foot 
health and wellness facility and related amenities.

Conditions of Closing The District’s obligation to gr ound lease the Property to the 
Developer shall be conditioned on the conditions to closing to be set 
forth in the Transaction Documents. 
Green Building 
Requirements 
The Developer shall construct the Project in compliance with the 
Green Building Act of 2006, D.C. Official Code §§ 6-1451.01 et 
seq., as amended, and the regulations promulgated therewith.   
Schedule of 
Performance 
The following contains terms of the Schedule of Performance with 
estimated dates, which may be amended and extended with the 
approval of DMPED in accordance with the terms of the Transaction 
Documents: 
• Closing – will occur prior to the expiration of disposition 
authority. 
• Commencement of Construction of the Project – will occur 
within ninety (90) days of the Closing. 
• Substantial Completion of the Project – will occur within 
sixty (60) months of Commencement of Construction.  
• Final Completion of the Project – will occur within twelve 
(12) months of Substantial Completion. 
Closing Requirements • The Transaction Documents will provide that the Developer 
shall execute at Closing a Construction and Use Covenant 
and shall be bound to comply w ith the requirements of the 
same for the applicable durations identified therein. 
• The Transaction Documents will provide that the Developer 
shall deliver to the District at Closing a Development and 
Completion Guaranty executed by a guarantor approved by 
DMPED. 
Certified Business 
Enterprise 
The Developer shall enter into a Certified Business Enterprise 
Utilization and Participation Agre ement with the Department of 
Small and Local Business Development governing certain 
contracting obligations of the Deve loper with respect  to the design 
and construction of the Projec t under the Small, Local and 
Disadvantaged Business Enterpri se Development and Assistance 
Act of 2005, as amended.   
First Source 
Requirements 
The Developer shall enter into a First Source Agreement with the 
Department of Employment Services that shall govern obligations of 
the Developer pursuant to D.C. Official Code §§ 2-219.03, et seq., 
and Mayor’s Order 83-265 (November 9, 1983) regarding job 
creation and employment generated as a result of the  design and 
construction of the Project.

INTENTION AND LIMITATIONS OF THIS TERM SHEET 
 
1. The Developer and DMPED acknowledge that th ey have prepared and signed this Term 
Sheet for the sole purpose of obtaining the approval of the Council of the District of 
Columbia (the “Council”) to the proposed transaction. The Developer acknowledges that 
DMPED's negotiation of the Transaction Docu ments and the preparation of this Term 
Sheet, DMPED’s signature on this Term Sheet,  and submission of this Term Sheet and 
supporting documents to the Council shall not bind the District to execute the Transaction 
Documents or to convey th e Property to the Develope r. The Developer further 
acknowledges that, notwithstanding Council au thorizing the conveyance of the Property, 
the District has no obligation to do so absent the District and the Developer duly executing 
the Transaction Documents and the satisfaction of the conditions contained therein. In the 
event DMPED or the Mayor determine, in thei r sole and absolute discretion, to withhold 
submission of this Term Sheet and supporting documents to the Council or to otherwise 
decline to secure C ouncil authorization for the c onveyance, DMPED may terminate 
negotiations with the Developer and the Di strict shall not be responsible for the 
Developer’s costs and expenses incurred in relation to the Property or the Project. 
 
2. The Developer acknowledges that all approvals required of the Council will be granted or 
withheld in the sole and absolute discretion of the Co uncil and that, absent Council 
approval of the proposed transaction, DMPED ha s no authority to convey the Property to 
the Developer. The Developer acknowledges that  it is signing this Term Sheet prior to 
obtaining all necessary Council approvals. In the absence of such approvals and execution 
of the Transaction Documents, the Developer proceeds at its sole risk and expense with no 
recourse whatsoever against the District.  
 
3. The Developer and DMPED agree that upon receipt of all necessary Council approvals, 
the Developer and DMPED intend to negotiate the Transaction Documents that will govern 
all of the terms and conditions of the conveyance of the Property.    
 
4. Upon the execution of the Transaction Docu ments, the Developer and DMPED shall 
proceed in accordance with the terms of th e Transaction Documents; provided, however, 
that the Developer and DMPED acknowledge and agree that any substantive change in the 
terms set forth in this Term Sheet shall be subject to further Council review and approval 
in accordance with the Council act authorizing this transaction.

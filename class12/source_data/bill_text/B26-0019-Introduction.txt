 Sec. 2. Chapter 46 of Title 47 of the District of Columbia Official Code is amended as 8 
follows: 9 
 (a) The table of contents is amended by adding a new section designation to read as 10 
follows: 11 
 “47-4681. Avanti Real Estate, Inc.; Lot 123, Square 2836.” 12 
  (b) A new section 47-4681 is added to read as follows: 13 
 “Sec. 47-4681. Avanti Real Estate, Inc.; Lot 123, Square 2836. 14 
 “(a)(1) The real property designated for tax purposes as Lot 123, Square 2836, located at 15 
3421 14th Street, N.W., ("Property"), which is owned by Avanti Real Estate Services, Inc., shall 16 
be exempt from the tax imposed by Chapter 8 of this title for the period beginning October 1, 17 
2024, and ending September 30, 2034; provided, that the property is occupied by Avanti Real 18

Estate Services, LLC and is used to further the purposes of providing Real Estate and Brokerage 19 
services, specializing in creating generational wealth through homeownership, employing 20 
District residents, and providing education and training for employment opportunities and 21 
careers within the real estate industry. 22 
  “(2) Any real property taxes, interest, penalties, fees, or other related charges 23 
assessed, as of the effective date of this act, against the property are forgiven. 24 
 “(b)(1) In each year of the exemption period, the Mayor shall certify to the Office of Tax 25 
and Revenue the Property's eligibility for the exemption provided pursuant to subsection (a) of 26 
this section. 27 
  “(2) If at any time the Mayor determines that the Property has become ineligible 28 
for the exemption provided pursuant to subsection (a) this section, the Mayor shall notify the 29 
Office of Tax and Revenue and shall specify the date that the Property became ineligible.” 30

Dear Secretary Howard: 
 
Today, I am introducing the “Closing of a Public Alley in Square 2068, S.O. 25-01801, Act of 
2025.” This legislation would close the 15-foot-wide L-shaped portion of the alley system in 
Square 2068 abutting Lots 92, 811, and 818 for purposes of facilitating the consolidation of 
properties and moving forward with construction by 3500 CT LLC and Uptown Heights LLC of 
a three-story, plus penthouse, residential building.  
 
The closing of this alley would allow for a more efficient site plan for the project, consisting of 
approximately 13,180 square feet of residential gross floor area and a parking area for 
approximately 12 to 14 vehicles. The portion of the alley being closed will not preclude access to 
services for the surrounding properties nor the project property given access to other alleys off 
Ordway and Porter streets. 
 
Should you have any questions about this legislation, please contact my Chief of Staff, Leigh 
Catherine Miles, at lcmiles@dccouncil.gov. 
 
Sincerely,

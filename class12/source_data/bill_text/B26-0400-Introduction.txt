To amend Chapter 23 of Title 16 of the District of Columbia Official Code to consolidate, 16 
standardize, and update the statutory definition of neglect; To amend Title 1 of the 17 
Prevention of Child Abuse and Neglect Act of 1977 to clarify that the Child and Family 18 
Services Agency shall not use a parent or guardian’s eligibility for or use of public 19 
benefits as the sole evidence that parental failure is unrelated to neglect and to clarify that 20 
homelessness or lack of stable housing shall not be used as sole evidence of neglect. 21 
 22 
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 23 
act may be cited as the “Statutory Neglect Amendment Act of 2025.” 24 
Sec. 2. Chapter 23 of Title 16 of the District of Columbia Official Code is amended as 25 
follows:   26 
(a) Section 16-2301(9)(A) is amended to read as follows: 27 
“(A) The term “neglected child” means a child: 28 
“(i) who has been abused by their parent, guardian, or custodian, or whose parent, 29 
guardian, or custodian has failed to take actions that are reasonable under the circumstances to 30 
protect the child from abuse; 31 
“(ii) who is suffering or there is a substantial risk that the child will suffer serious 32 
physical, mental or emotional harm or illness, based on consideration of the totality of the 33 
circumstances, as a result of any of the following:  34

“(I) The failure of the child’s parent, guardian, or custodian to plan for or 35 
provide adequate supervision for the child, after considering such factors as the child's age, 36 
mental ability, physical condition, and environment; or  37 
“(II) The failure of the child’s parent, guardian, or custodian to plan for or 38 
provide the child with adequate food, clothing, or shelter; education; or treatment or care 39 
necessary for the child’s physical or mental health, provided the failure is not due solely to lack 40 
of financial means, demonstrated by reasonable efforts by the Child and Family Services Agency 41 
pursuant to §4–1301.09a. 42 
“(iii) who has been abandoned;  43 
“(iv) whose parent, guardian, or custodian refuses, is unavailable, or is unable to 44 
continue providing for or assuming the child’s care, control, or subsistence;  45 
“(v) who is in imminent danger of being abused and another child living in the 46 
same household or under the care of the same parent, guardian, or custodian has been abused; 47 
“(vi) who is born dependent on a controlled substance or has a significant 48 
presence of a controlled substance their system at birth;  49 
“(vii) in whose body there is a controlled substance as a direct and foreseeable 50 
consequence of the acts or omissions of the child’s parent, guardian, or custodian; 51 
“(viii) who is regularly exposed to illegal drug-related activity in the home; 52 
“(ix) who has been subjected to, or is in imminent danger of being subjected to, 53 
female genital mutilation pursuant to § 22-1431(b); or 54 
“(x) whose parent, guardian, or custodian can give no satisfactory explanation for 55 
evidence of illness or injury to the child.”. 56 
(b) Section 16-2301(23)(A) is amended to read as follows:  57

“(A) The term “abused” when used with reference to a child, means: 58 
“(i) infliction of physical injury; 59 
“(ii) infliction of mental injury; 60 
“(iii) sexual abuse or exploitation; 61 
“(iv) exposure to domestic violence as defined by section § 4-551; 62 
“(v) mistreatment of the child by withholding adequate food, clothing, shelter, or 63 
medical care; 64 
“(vi) interfering with a child's breathing; or 65 
“(vii) threatening a child with a firearm, a knife, or any of the prohibited weapons 66 
described in § 22-4514.”. 67 
(c) Section 16-2301(23)(B)(i) is amended to read as follows:  68 
“(i) For the purposes of this subchapter, the term “abuse” does not include physical 69 
discipline administered by a parent, guardian, or custodian to their child, provided that the child 70 
is at least 3 years of age, and the physical discipline is reasonable in manner and moderate in 71 
degree.”. 72 
(d) A new Section 16-2301(52) is added to read as follows: 73 
“(52)(A) The term “abandoned” means a child:  74 
“(I) whose parent, guardian, or legal custodian has not made reasonable 75 
efforts to maintain a parental relationship with the child for a period of at least four (4) months; 76 
or 77 
“(II) who has resided in a hospital located in the District of Columbia for 78 
at least 10 days following the birth of the child and the parent of the child did not undertake any 79 
action or make any effort to maintain a parental relationship or contact with the child or hospital. 80

“(B) A child who is surrendered pursuant to the District’s Safe Haven law as pursuant to 81 
§ 4–1451 shall not be considered abandoned.”. 82 
(c) Paragraph (24) is stricken. 83 
Sec. 3. Title 1 of the Prevention of Child Abuse and Neglect Act of 1977, effective 84 
September 23, 1977 (D.C. Law 2-22; D.C. Official Code § 4-1301.04 et seq.), is amended as 85 
follows: 86 
(a) New subsections 106(b-1) and 106(b-2) are added to read as follows:  87 
“(b-1) A finding by the Agency of a parent or guardian’s eligibility for or receipt of 88 
public benefits shall not be used by the Agency as the sole basis to establish that parental failure 89 
is unrelated to a lack of financial means under §16-2301(9)(A)(ii). 90 
“(b-2) Homelessness or lack of stable housing shall not be used by the Agency as the sole 91 
basis for substantiating neglect under § 16-2301(9)(A).” 92

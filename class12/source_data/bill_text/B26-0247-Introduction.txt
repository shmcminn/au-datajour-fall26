To amend the Business Improvement District Acts of 1996 to permit the Mayor to approve a  16 
petition by the Anacostia BID to expand its geographic area to include exempt real  17

 19 
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this  20 
act may be cited as the “Anacostia Business Improvement Districts Amendment Act of 2025. 21 
Sec. 2.  Section 21 of the Business Improvement Districts Act of 1996, effective May 29,  22 
1996 (D.C. Law 11-134; D.C. Official Code § 2-1215.21 et seq.), is amended as follows:  23 
(a) Designate the existing text as subsection (a).  24 
(b) A new subsection (b) is added to read as follows:  25 
“(b) The Mayor may approve a petition by the Anacostia BID to expand its 26 
geographic area to include exempt real property owners adjacent to or abutting the Anacostia  27 
BID’s geographical area as delineated in DC Code §2-1215.59 .”.  28 
Sec. 3. Fiscal impact statement.  29 
The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement in the committee report as the  30 
fiscal impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act  31 
of 1975, approved October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D. C. Official Code § 1-301.47a). 32

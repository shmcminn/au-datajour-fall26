To amend the Department of Youth Rehabilitation Services Establishment Act of 2004 to require 24 
the Department to develop policies for and provide prenatal and postpartum care to 25 
pregnant youth in its care.  26 
 27 
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 28 
act may be cited as the “Safe Pregnancy, Delivery, and Postpartum Care for Youth at DYRS 29 
Amendment Act of 2025”.  30 
 Sec. 2. The Department of Youth Rehabilitation Services Establishment Act of 2004, 31 
effective April 12, 2005 (D.C. Law 15-335; D.C. Official Code § 2-1515.01 et seq.) is amended 32 
by inserting a new section 111 to read as follows: 33 
 “Sec. 111. Safe and supported pregnancy and delivery for detained youth. 34

2 
“(a) The Department shall make available a pregnancy test to all female youth upon 35 
admission to a facility under its control and additionally when required as a baseline assessment 36 
for prescription of a recommended medication by a physician. 37 
“(b) Upon confirmation of a detained youth’s pregnancy, the Department shall advise the 38 
youth orally or in writing, of the standards and policies governing pregnant youth in the 39 
Department's care. 40 
“(c) The Department shall ensure that pregnant youth in its care receive confidential, 41 
appropriate, timely, culturally responsive, and comprehensive healthcare, evaluation, and 42 
treatment from maternal healthcare providers licensed in the District, including: 43 
“(1) Healthcare for conditions that emerge during pregnancy or postpartum, 44 
including contact with a maternal care provider within the first 3 weeks postpartum followed by 45 
ongoing care as needed, concluding with a comprehensive postpartum visit no later than 12 46 
weeks after birth; 47 
“(2) Specialized pregnancy or postpartum healthcare, if necessary, which may be 48 
referred to external healthcare providers, in accordance with the Limitations on the Use of 49 
Restraints Amendment Act of 2014, effective July 25, 2015 (D.C. Law 20-280; D.C. Official 50 
Code 24-276.01 et seq.); 51 
“(3) Counseling regarding:  52 
“(A) Family planning; 53 
“(B) Prenatal Counseling and Education; 54 
“(C) Birth control; 55 
“(D) Test results; and 56 
“(E) Counseling for perinatal mental health conditions. 57

3 
“(4) Medical, healthcare, and personal care supplies prescribed or recommended 58 
by a licensed healthcare provider treating an individual or reasonably requested by an individual, 59 
which shall be provided at no cost to the individual, including: 60 
“(A) Equipment and cleaning supplies necessary to safely pump, store, 61 
and transport breast milk, including nursing brassieres, breast pumps, breast milk storage bags, 62 
detergent, bottle brushes, drying racks, a refrigerator of sufficient capacity, and a cooler; and 63 
“(B) The diet that is required by Department policy or is medically 64 
recommended during pregnancy and postpartum. 65 
“(d) No later than 60 days following enactment of the Safe Pregnancy, Delivery, and 66 
Postpartum Care for Youth at DYRS Amendment Act of 2025, the Department shall develop and 67 
deliver to Council written policies and procedures pertaining to pregnant youth in its facilities 68 
that address the requirements in this section.”. 69 
Sec. 3. Fiscal impact statement. 70 
The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement in the committee report as the fiscal 71 
impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975, 72 
approved October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a). 73 
Sec. 4. Effective date. 74 
This act shall take effect after approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the 75 
Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), a 30-day period of congressional review as 76 
provided in section 602(c)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December 77 
24, 1973 (87 Stat. 813; D.C. Official Code § 1-206.02(c)(1)), and publication in the District of 78 
Columbia Register. 79

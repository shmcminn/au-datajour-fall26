To amend the method for calculating individual school budgets within the District of Columbia 12 
Public Schools System by adding average position cost as a factor.  13 
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 14 
act may be cited as the “Schools First Amendment Act of 2025”.   15 
Sec. 2. The Schools First in Budgeting Amendment Act of 2022, effective March 10, 16 
2023 (D.C Law 24-300; D.C. Official Code § 38-2851.01 et seq.), is amended as follows:  17 
(a) Section 102 (D.C. Official Code § 38-2851.01) is amended as follows: 18 
 (1) Paragraph (1A) is redesignated as paragraph (1B) 19 
 (2) A new paragraph (1A) is added to read as follows: 20 
  “(1A) “Average position cost for educators” means the cost, including salary, of a 21 
school-based educator that DCPS uses to calculate each school’s annual budget for the next 22 
fiscal year.”.  23 
(b) Section 104(a) (D.C. Official Code § 38-2851.03(a)), is amended as follows:  24 
 (1) A new paragraph (5A) is added to read as follows:  25 
  “(5A) Increase the amount derived pursuant to paragraph (5) of this subsection by 26 
the projected increase over the previous fiscal year in the average position cost for Washington 27 
Teacher Union members.”. 28 
  (2) Paragraph (6) is amended by striking the phrase “paragraph (5)” and inserting 29 
the phrase “paragraph (5A)” in its place.  30

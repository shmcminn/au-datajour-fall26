To amend the Health Services Planning Program Re-establishment Act of 1996 to exempt from 24 
the certificate of need process digital-only telehealth platforms and providers and primary 25 
care and specialty care providers not affiliated with hospitals and medical schools, to 26 
define group practice and nonpatient care project, to remove the 3 year maximum on 27 
certificate of need applications for active projects, to require the State Health Planning 28 
and Development Agency to update the capital expenditure and medical equipment 29 
spending thresholds every two years, to increase the number of beds that must be added 30 
or removed at a health care facility in order to trigger a certificate of need, and to require 31 
the Department of Health to create a registration process for facilities offering primary 32 
and secondary care, virtual provider networks, and virtual telehealth platforms. 33 
 34 
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 35 
act may be cited as the “Certificate of Need Improvement Amendment Act of 2025.” 36

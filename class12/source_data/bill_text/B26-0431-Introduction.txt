To name the basketball court at the Marvin Gaye Recreation Center, located at 15 61st Street, 16 
NE, the Rodney Wright Basketball Court to commemorate the life of Rodney Wright, a 17 
native Washingtonian and 1997 all-Metropolitan pick who led the Howard Bisons to two 18 
MEAC titles before becoming head coach at Eastern High School, where he was twice 19 
named DCPS Coach of the Year. 20 
 21 
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 22 
act may be cited as the “Rodney Wright Basketball Court Designation Act of 2025”. 23 
Sec. 2. Pursuant to section 401 of the Street and Alley Closing and Acquisitions 24 
Procedures Act of 1982, effective March 10, 1983 (D.C. Law 4-201; D.C. Official Code § 9-25 
204.01), the basketball court at the Marvin Gaye Recreation Center is hereby named the “Rodney 26 
Wright Basketball Court”. 27 
Sec. 3. The Secretary to the Council shall transmit a copy of this act, after it becomes 28 
effective, to the Director of the Department of Parks and Recreation. 29

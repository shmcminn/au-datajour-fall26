To amend the Historic Landmark and Historic District Protection Act of 1978 to clarify that 1 
review of new construction by the Historic Preservation Review Board shall not result in 2 
the reduction of housing units or density below what is permitted under zoning.  3 
 4 
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 5 
act may be cited as the “Housing Capacity Preservation Amendment Act of 2025”. 6 
 Sec. 2. Section 7 of the Historic Landmark and Historic District Protection Act of 1978, 7 
effective March 3, 1979 (D.C. Law 2-144; D.C. Official Code § 6-1101 et seq.) is amended as 8 
follows:  9 
 (a) Section 3(4) (D.C. Official Code § 6-1102(4)) is amended by striking the phrase 10 
“height,”. 11 
 Section 7 (D.C. Official Code § 6-1107) is amended by adding a new subsection (g) to 12 
read as follows:  13

2 
 “(g) A finding of incompatibility pursuant to subsection (f) of this section shall not be 14 
based on a finding that would necessitate a reduction of the floor area ratio, residential square 15

Adjustment.”. 19

To amend section § 47-850 of the District of Columbia Official Code to provide that a surviving 1 
spouse or domestic partner of a veteran may claim the Disabled Veterans Homestead 2 
Exemption. 3 
 4 
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 5 
act may be cited as the “Spousal Homestead Exemption Amendment Act of 2025”.  6 
 Sec. 2. Section § 47-850 of the District of Columbia Official Code is amended as follows: 7 
 (a) A new paragraph 4 is added to read as follows: 8 
  “(4) For the purposes of this subsection, the term “eligible spouse” means the  9 
surviving spouse or domestic partner of a veteran who was receiving the benefits provided under 10 
this section prior to passing or who would have been eligible for the benefits prior to passing.”. 11 
 (b) Strike the word “veteran” wherever it appears and insert the phrase “veteran or  12 
eligible spouse” in its place. 13 
 Sec. 3. Applicability.  14 
 This act shall apply as of October 1, 2022. 15

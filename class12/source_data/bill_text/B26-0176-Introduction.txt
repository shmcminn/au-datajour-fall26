 “Sec. 15c. Early Childhood Educator Equitable Compensation Task Force establishment. 156 
 “(a)(1) There is established by the Council an Early Childhood Educator Equitable 157

employee compensation scale for child development facilities. 159 
  “(2) Following the submission of the report required pursuant to subsection (c)(3) 160 
of this section, the Task Force shall reconvene every 4th calendar year, or as deemed necessary 161 
by the Chairman of the Council. 162

   “(A) Families whose children are receiving or have received childcare 166 
services from an early childhood development provider in the District; 167 
   “(B) Community-based organizations; 168 
   “(C) Early childhood advocacy organizations; 169 
   “(D) Operators of child development facilities who participate in the 170 
childcare subsidy program; 171 
   “(E) Operators of child development facilities who do not currently 172 
participate in the childcare subsidy program; 173 
   “(F) Operators of home-based child development facilities; 174 
   “(G) Employees of child development facilities; and 175 
   “(H) An individual with an expertise in economics or policy, who has an 176 
understanding of the District’s early childhood development and education sector.  177 
  “(3) At least 2 members of the Task Force shall be employees of child 178 
development facilities.  179 
  “(4) The Chairman, or his or her designee, shall serve as the Chairperson of the 180 
Task Force. 181

 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 19 
act may be cited as the “Alpha Omega Social Action and Scholarship Foundation Real Property 20 
Tax Exemption Act of 2026”. 21 
 Sec. 2. Chapter 10 of Title 47 of the District of Columbia Official Code is amended as 22 
follows: 23 
 (a) The table of contents is amended by adding a new section designation to read as 24 
follows: 25 
 “47-1099.17. Alpha Omega Social Action and Scholarship Foundation; Lot 813, Square 26 
2853.”. 27 
 (b) A new section 47-1099.17 is added to read as follows: 28 
 “§ 47-1099.17. Alpha Omega Social Action and Scholarship Foundation; Lot 813, Square 29 
2853. 30 
 “(a) The real property, and any improvements on the property, located at 1231 Harvard 31 
Street, N.W., known for tax and assessment purposes as Lot 813, Square 2853 (“Property”), shall 32

2 
 
be exempt from the tax imposed by Chapter 8 of this title; provided that the Property is owned by 33 
Alpha Omega Social Action and Scholarship Foundation; and provided further, that the Property 34 
is used for its charitable and educational purposes, and is not used for commercial purposes. 35 
 “(b) The tax exemption provided pursuant to this section shall be in addition to and not in 36 
lieu of any other tax relief or assistance from any other source applicable to Alpha Omega Social 37 
Action and Scholarship Foundation.”. 38 
 Sec. 3. The Council of the District of Columbia orders that all real property taxes, 39 
interest, penalties, fees, and other related charges assessed against Lot 813, Square 2853 for the 40 
period between October 1, 2018 and September 30, 2026, be forgiven. Any payments of real 41 
property taxes, interest, penalties, fees, or other related charges made prior to the effective date 42 
of this act shall be refunded to the person who made the payments no later than 45 days after the 43 
effect date of this act. 44 
 Sec. 4. Fiscal impact statement. 45 
 The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement in the committee report as the fiscal 46

 Sec. 3. Findings 99

5 
 
(a) The RFK Campus consists of approximately 180 acres of waterfront property located 100 
in the District within Reservation 343F, as shown on the transfer of jurisdiction plat recorded in 101 
the Surveyor’s Office on February 5, 2025, on page 13 of subdivision book 223. The District has 102 
been unable to redevelop the site over the last decade due to federal restrictions on development 103 
at the site, and the site has remained vacant, blighted, and fallow. Over the course of the last 104 
decade, the District has worked to gain broader control of the site and remove the federal 105 
development restrictions so that a new, multi-use future for the area could be realized. 106 
 (b) With bipartisan support from Congress, the President signed Public Law 118-274, the 107 
D.C. Robert F. Kennedy Memorial Stadium Campus Revitalization Act, into law on January 6, 108 
2025. Pursuant to this law, the National Park Service transferred administrative jurisdiction over 109 
the RFK Campus to the District. The Council approved the transfer of jurisdiction pursuant to the 110 
Transfer of Jurisdiction Over the Robert F. Kennedy Memorial Stadium Campus Emergency 111 
Approval Resolution of 2025, effective February 4, 2025 (Res. 26-16; 72 DCR 1436). 112 
 (c) The District now has a once-in-a-generation opportunity to continue to revitalize the 113 
Anacostia Waterfront, catalyze growth, and develop a vibrant, mixed-use development featuring 114 
community sports, professional sports, public recreation, public park space, and cultural 115 
amenities. The development will provide the District with significant economic, cultural, social, 116 
and financial benefits. 117 
 (d) Since gaining administrative jurisdiction over the RFK Campus, the District 118 
government has undertaken a comprehensive public engagement process, considering the 119 
specific interests of communities near the RFK Campus; economic development opportunities; 120 
the capacity of the campus to generate jobs for District residents and opportunities for District 121 
businesses; park, open space, and recreational options; river access options; potential community 122

6 
 
amenities; opportunities to create new housing units; and environmental and historical factors, 123 
among other considerations, to determine the best uses for the site. 124 
 (e) Considering the public input received, the existing infrastructure of the campus, and 125 
the possibility of a catalytic investment in the District, it was determined that the construction 126 
and operation of a new first-class, state-of-the-art, sports stadium with supporting facilities, 127 
including parking, and infrastructure, for use by the professional football franchise owned by 128 
Pro-Football LLC, and an adjacent Sportsplex would be of considerable public benefit to the 129 
District as the first stage of the redevelopment and revitalization of the entire campus. 130 
 (f) To ensure delivery of the Stadium, supporting facilities, and Sportsplex by 2030, a 131 
limited zoning exemption is necessary for the RFK Campus. 132 
 (g) Planning for other residential and commercial development at the RFK Campus, in 133 
addition to other uses permitted by the transfer of jurisdiction and the Declaration of Covenants, 134 
will continue as the Stadium and Sportsplex projects move forward, and will inform the 135 
Comprehensive Plan rewrite (DC 2050). 136 
 (h) The District intends for the comprehensive redevelopment of the RFK Campus to 137 
create a vibrant, multi-use community and destination that will create new jobs, business 138 
opportunities, housing, community amenities, and neighborhood-serving retail and achieve the 5 139 
Anacostia Waterfront Framework Plan themes: (1) a clean and active river, (2) gaining access to, 140 
along, and across the river, (3) a great riverfront park system, (4) a riverfront of distinct places 141 
and cultural destinations, and (5) building and sustaining strong waterfront neighborhoods. 142 
 (i) The District and Pro-Football LLC have entered into a term sheet for the development 143 
of the Stadium and supporting facilities and the redevelopment of a portion of the RFK Campus 144 
that sets forth the terms and conditions upon which the parties are willing to undertake the 145 
projects. 146

7 
 
 (j) The construction, development, and revitalization of the RFK Campus and the public 147 
ownership of the Stadium, for use for athletic and entertainment events, is a municipal use that is 148 
in the interest of, and for the benefit of, District residents because such a publicly-owned 149 
Stadium will contribute to the social and economic well-being of District residents and 150 
significantly enhance economic development and employment opportunities in the District. 151 
 (k) To further that interest, it is appropriate for the District to take certain actions with 152 
respect to the redevelopment of the RFK Campus, as further set forth in this act. 153

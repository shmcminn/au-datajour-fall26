To amend the Advisory Neighborhood Commissions Act of 1975 to authorize Advisory 15 
Neighborhood Commissions to expend funds through certain third-party digital payment 16 
platforms. 17 
 18 
BE IT ENACTED by the Council of the District of Columbia, that this act may be cited as the 19 
“Advisory Neighborhood Commissions Payment Modernization Act of 2026.” 20 
Sec. 2. Section 16 of the Advisory Neighborhood Commissions Act of 1975, effective 21 
March 26, 1976 (D.C. Law 1-58; D.C. Official Code § 1-309.13), is amended as follows: 22 
Section 16 of the Advisory Neighborhood Commissions Act of 1975, effective March 26, 23 
1976 (D.C. Law 1-58; D.C. Official Code § 1-309.13), is amended as follows: 24 
(a) Subsection (b-2) is amended as follows: 25 
(1) Paragraph (1) is amended by striking the phrase "payments." and adding the 26 
phrase "payments, and by transfers made through a third-party digital payment platform." 27 
(2) Paragraph (2) is amended to read as follows 28 
“(2) Each Commission expending funds by EFT, ACH payments, or a third-party 29 
digital payment platform shall do so pursuant to a procedure determined by the OANC that limits 30 
monthly EFT, ACH payments, or a third-party digital payment platform expenditures relative to 31 
the Commission's quarterly allotment.” 32

(3) Paragraph (3) is amended by striking the phrase "EFT or ACH payments" and 33 
adding the phrase "EFT or ACH payments, or transaction identifiers assigned by a third-party 34 
digital payment platform” 35 
(b) Subsection (f) is amended as follows: 36 
(1) Paragraph (2A)(A) is amended by striking the phrase "debit card or ACH" and 37 
adding the phrase "debit card, ACH, or a third-party digital payment platform". 38 
(c) A new subsection (t-1) is added to read as follows: 39 
"(t-1)(1) For the purposes of this section, the term "third-party digital payment 40 
platform" means a payment service, accessible by website or mobile application, that: 41 
(A) Allows electronic transfers of Commission funds from a depository 42 
account or debit card to a vendor, grant recipient, or Commissioner; 43 
(B) Provides the Commission with itemized transaction records that 44 
include, at a minimum, the date, amount, payee, and an associated transaction identifier for each 45 
transfer; and 46 
(C) Allows the Commission to associate each transfer with a description 47 
or memo field stating the purpose of the expenditure. 48 
Sec. 3. Fiscal impact statement. 49 
The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement in the committee report as the fiscal 50 
impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975, 51 
approved October 16, 2006 (D.C. Law 16-191; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a). 52 
Sec. 4. Effective date. 53 
This act shall take effect following approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the 54 
Mayor, action by the Council to override the veto), a 30-day period of Congressional review as 55

provided in section 602(c)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December 56 
24, 1973 (87 Stat. 813; D.C. Official Code § 1-206.02(c)(1)), and publication in the District of 57 
Columbia Register. 58

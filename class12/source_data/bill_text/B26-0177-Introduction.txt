To amend the Rental Housing Act of 1985 to prevent housing providers from evicting 17 
tenants on any day when the temperature is predicted to be above 95 degrees. 18 
 19 
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 20 
act may be cited as the “Extreme Heat Eviction Protection Amendment Act of 2025”.  21 
Sec. 2. Section 501(k) of the Rental Housing Act of 1985, effective July 17, 1985 22 
(D.C. Law 6-10; D.C. Code § 42-3505.01(k)), is amended as follows: 23 
(a) Strike the phrase “Celsius; or” and insert the phrase “Celsius;” in its place. 24 
(b) Strike the phrase “rental unit.” and insert the phrase “rental unit; or” in its 25 
place. 26 
(c) Add a new paragraph (3) to read as follows: 27 
“(3) On any day when the National Weather Service predicts at 8:00 a.m. the 28 
temperature at the National Airport weather station will rise above 95 degrees Fahrenheit 29 
or 35 degrees Celsius.”. 30

To amend the Advisory Neighborhood Councils Act of 1975 to provide an opportunity for the  12 
Office of Advisory Neighborhood Commissions to evaluate any proposal to establish 13 
single-member districts to ensure the neighborhood cohesiveness and viability of such 14 
districts, and for the OANC to submit a report to the Council on its findings and 15 
recommendations, to require the Council to consider the report of the OANC when 16 
making adjustments in the boundaries of the Advisory Neighborhood Commission 17 
single-member districts and ANC areas, to provide for the sufficiency of a certification 18 
by the Department of Corrections that a Commissioner who is a resident of the Central 19 
Detention Facility or the Correctional Treatment Facility has been transferred or is 20 
otherwise no longer a resident of the facility in order to declare the seat vacant, to 21 
provide that the Board of Elections may accept a funeral home death notice or obituary, 22 
or an obituary published in a newspaper of general circulation or any other proof of 23 
death that the Board deems sufficient in order to declare the seat vacant, to clarify that if 24 
a single-member district contains the CDF and CTF, the Board of Elections shall make 25 
in-person voting available to qualified registered electors who are not in the care and 26 
custody of DOC, that the Department of Corrections shall make voting available to 27 
registered qualified electors in its care and custody in consultation with the Board of 28 
Elections, that the Board of Elections shall transmit the results of the voting to the 29 
OANC and the affected ANC, and that ballot counting for the voting shall be facilitated 30 
by at least two representatives of the Board of Elections, to correct an erroneous citation 31 
in Section 13 regarding reference to the ability of an ANC to object to a license for the 32 
sale or on-premises consumption of alcohol, to repeal a provision regarding the 33 
discretionary requirement for a Commission to final a fiscal year annual report with the 34 
Council and the Mayor, to add a provision that quarterly allotments will be withheld 35 
from any Commission without an approved annual fiscal year spending plan by 36 
November 30th of each year until such time as an annual budget is approved and filed 37 
with the OANC, to clarify that no Commission may solicit donations unless specifically 38 
authorized, to provide that a copy of each Commission’s bylaws and all amendments 39 
thereto shall not be effective until transmittal to the Council and the OANC and posting 40 
on the OANC website, to provide that the Commission vice-chairperson shall schedule a 41 
special meeting to remove an officer after a proper request for removal by the 42 
Commission is made if the Chairperson does not schedule a meeting within 30 days of 43 
the request, to clarify that the OANC shall provide advice regarding Commission 44 
expenditures and responses to inquiries from Commissions on the legality of proposed 45 
actual expenditures, to require that any release of ANC allotment funding which will 46

To amend the District of Columbia Government Comprehensive Merit Personnel Act of 1978 to 18 
increase the compensation of the Mayor, Chairman of the Council, and Attorney General 19 
for the District of Columbia; and to provide for cost-of-living increases as part of the 20 
compensation for the Mayor.   21 
 22 
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 23 
act may be cited as the “Salary Adjustment Amendment Act of 2026.” 24 
Sec. 2. Section 1109(a-1) of the District of Columbia Government Comprehensive Merit 25 
Personnel Act of 1978, effective March 3, 1979 (D.C. Law 2-139; D.C. Official Code § 1-26 
611.09(a-1), is amended as follows: 27 
 (1) By striking the phrase “January 2, 2023, the Mayor shall receive annual 28 
compensation in the amount of $250,000,” and inserting the phrase “January 2, 2027, the Mayor 29 
shall receive annual compensation in the amount of $290,000,” in its place; and 30 
 (2) By striking the phrase “The compensation shall not be subject to step, cost of 31 
living, or other increases” and inserting the phrase “The compensation shall be subject to cost-of-32 
living increases, but not to step or other increases” in its place. 33

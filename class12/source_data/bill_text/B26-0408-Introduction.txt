To amend Chapter 23 of Title 16 of the Official Code of the District of Columbia to require 25 
DYRS to maintain an electronic record of its attempts to contact the appropriate parties, 26 
including parents and guardians, prior to a predisposition meeting and to make this log 27 
available to the Chair of the Council committee with oversight of the DYRS. 28 
 29 
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 30 
act may be cited as the “Promoting Parental Engagement at DYRS Amendment Act of 2025”. 31 
Sec. 2. Chapter 23 of Title 16 of the District of Columbia Official Code is amended as 32 
follows: 33 
“(a) Section 16-2319(d)(1)(B) is amended to read as follows: 34 
“(B) Prior to the predisposition hearing meeting required by subparagraph (C) of this 35 
paragraph, make reasonable efforts to notify the child, the child’s attorney, the child’s parent, 36 
guardian, or custodian, a Department representative, a Court Social Services representative, a 37

2 
 
Child and Family Services Agency representative, if relevant, and any other individual requested 38 
by the Department, of the date, time, and location of the predisposition meeting. Following the 39 
date of this scheduled predisposition meeting, the Department shall make a recurring effort at 40 
least every six (6) months to contact the child’s parent, guardian, or custodian and inform them 41 
of the child’s progress on their individualized rehabilitation plan. The Department shall keep an 42 
electronic log of all reasonable efforts made to contact the child’s parent, guardian, or custodian 43 
and shall provide this electronic log to the Chair of the Council committee with oversight of the 44 
Department upon request.”. 45 
Sec. 3. Fiscal impact statement. 46 
The Council adopts the fiscal impact statement in the committee report as the fiscal 47 
impact statement required by section 4a of the General Legislative Procedures Act of 1975, 48 
approved October 16, 2006 (120 Stat. 2038; D.C. Official Code § 1-301.47a). 49 
 Sec. 4. Effective date. 50 
 This act shall take effect after approval by the Mayor (or in the event of veto by the 51

provided in section 602(c)(1) of the District of Columbia Home Rule Act, approved December 53 
24, 1973 (87 Stat. 813; D.C. Official Code § 1-206.02(c)(1)), and publication in the District of 54 
Columbia Register. 55

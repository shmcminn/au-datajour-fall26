To amend the District of Columbia Revenue Act of 1937 to authorize the Mayor to design and 16 
issue motor vehicle identification tags with a design, identifying words, or emblem that 17 
demonstrates support for the return of the Washington Commanders football team to the 18 
District, and to direct that the proceeds be used to support the State Athletic Activities, 19 
Programs, and Office Fund; and to make a conforming amendment.  20 
 21 
 BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 22 
act may be cited as the “Washington Commanders Motor Vehicle Identification Tags 23 
Amendment Act of 2025”.  24 
 Sec. 2. Title IV of the District of Columbia Revenue Act of 1937, approved August 17, 25 
1937 (50 Stat. 679; D.C. Official Code § 50-1501.01 et seq.), is amended as follows: 26 
 (a) A new section 2o is added to read as follows:  27 
 “Sec. 2o. Issuance of Washington Commanders motor vehicle identification tags. 28 
 “(a) The Mayor may design and make available for issue one or more Washington 29 
Commanders motor vehicle identification tags to demonstrate support for the return of the 30 
Washington Commanders football team to the District.  31

 “(b)(1) A resident ordering a Washington Commanders tag shall pay a one-time 32

  "(2) The application and annual display fees shall be deposited into the State 35 
Athletic Activities, Programs, and Office Fund, established by § 38–2672. 36

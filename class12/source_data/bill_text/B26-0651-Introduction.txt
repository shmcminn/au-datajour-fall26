To amend the Robert F. Kennedy Memorial Stadium and the District of Columbia National 1 
Guard Armory Public Safety Act of 1977, to include the Downtown Arena and the 2 
Southeast Arena in prohibition on unauthorized entry onto a playing surface.  3 
 4 
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 5 
act may be cited as the "Arena Safety Conformity Amendment Act of 2026”. 6 
Sec. 2. The Robert F. Kennedy Memorial Stadium and the District of Columbia National 7 
Guard Armory Public Safety Act of 1977, effective November 11, 1977, (D.C. Law 2-37; D.C. 8 
Official Code § 3-341 et seq.), is amended as follows:   9 
(a) Section 3a (D.C. Official Code § 3-342.01) is amended as follows:   10 
(1) A new paragraph (1A) is added to read as follows:  11 
“(1A) “Downtown Arena” means a sports and entertainment arena located at 601 12 
F St. N.W.”.  13 
(2) A new paragraph (3) is added to read as follows:  14 
“(3) “Southeast Arena” means a sports and entertainment arena located at 1100 15 
Oak Dr. S.E.”.   16 
(b) Section 4b (D.C. Official Code § 3-343.02) is amended as follows:   17 
(1) New subsections (b-2) and (b-3) are added to read as follows:   18

2 
“(b-2) Unless expressly authorized by the operator of the Downtown Arena, or its duly 19 
authorized agents, no person shall at any time enter onto any portion of the playing surface 20 
within the Downtown Arena.”.  21 
“(b-3) Unless expressly authorized by the Washington Convention and Sports Authority, 22 
the lessee or operator of the Southeast Arena, or their duly authorized agents, no person shall at 23 
any time enter onto any portion of the playing surface within the Southeast Arena.”.  24 
(2) Subsection (c) is amended to read as follows:   25 
“(c) For the purposes of this section, the term “playing field” or “playing surface”, as 26 
applicable, means the area encompassed by the seating facilities within the Robert F. Kennedy 27 
Memorial Stadium, the Baseball Stadium, the Soccer Stadium, the Downtown Arena, or the 28 
Southeast Arena, as such seating facilities may be arranged from time to time.”.   29

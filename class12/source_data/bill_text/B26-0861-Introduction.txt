Sec. 2. Disposition authority.  25 
(a)  Notwithstanding An Act Authorizing the sale of certain real estate in the District 26 
of Columbia no longer required for public purposes, approved August 5, 1939 (53 Stat. 1211; 27 
D.C. Official Code § 10-801 et seq.), or other provision of law, the Council authorizes the Mayor 28 
to dispose of the following real properties:  29 
  (1)  The real property located beneath Dupont Circle containing 30 
approximately 75,000 square feet of the subterranean space beneath the circular roadway 31 
encompassing Federal Reservation 60 (Square 114-E, Lot 800), the associated subterranean track 32 
tunnels and ramps north and south, the stairwells and access or egress associated with said 33 
subterranean space, commonly known as Dupont Underground, by amending the existing lease 34

with DCenter Inc., d/b/a Arts Coalition for Dupont Underground to extend the term, which 35 
currently expires on November 18, 2029, to expire on November 18, 2042.  36 
  (2)  The real property located at 1900 M Street, S.E.  (Square 1080E, Lot 37 
0803), commonly known as the Anacostia Boathouse site, by amending the existing lease with 38 
The Anacostia Community Boathouse Association, Inc., to extend the term, which currently 39 
expires on June 7, 2030, to expire on June 7, 2047.   40

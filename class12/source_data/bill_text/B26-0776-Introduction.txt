TITLE I. DESIGNATION OF THE DISTRICT AS THE TECH FOR GOOD 24 
CAPITAL. 25 
Sec. 101. Short title. 26 
This title may be cited as the “Sense of the Council on the Designation of the District of 27 
Columbia as the Tech for Good Capital Act of 2026”. 28 
Sec. 102. The Council finds that: 29 
(1) The District’s economy has long been closely tied to the federal government, making 30 
it particularly vulnerable to federal workforce reductions and broader shifts in federal 31 
employment and spending. 32 
(2) Recent federal workforce displacement and economic uncertainty have underscored 33

2 
 
the need for the District to diversify and strengthen its private-sector economy while creating 34 
new pathways to economic opportunity for District residents. 35 
(3) As the nation’s capital with one of the most highly educated workforces in the 36 
country, the District has a concentration of public-sector expertise, mission-driven organizations, 37 
and innovation-focused institutions whose work focuses on solving public problems. These 38 
unique assets best position the District to lead the development, testing, and scaling of 39 
technologies and innovations that address the nation’s most pressing public-interest challenges. 40 
(4) Establishing a clear economic identity around mission-driven innovation can help 41 
attract businesses, entrepreneurs, researchers, investors, and workers while strengthening the 42 
District’s long-term economic competitiveness. 43 
Sec. 103. It is the sense of the Council that the District of Columbia shall be designated 44 
as the nation’s “Tech for Good Capital”. 45

TITLE III. TECH FOR GOOD INNOVATION CLUSTERS. 196 
Sec. 301. Short title. 197 
This title may be cited as the “Tech for Good Innovation Clusters Act of 2026”. 198 
Sec. 302. Tech for Good innovation clusters. 199 
(a) The Deputy Mayor for Planning and Economic Development may convene or support 200 
innovation clusters related to public-interest technology as defined in D.C. Official Code § 47-201 
1818.21(5). 202 
(b) Innovation clusters established or supported pursuant to this section may include 203 
participation by: 204 
(1) Qualified public-interest technology companies, as defined in D.C. Official 205 
Code § 47-1818.21(6); 206 
(2) Institutions of higher education; 207 
(3) Workforce development providers; 208 
(4) 501(c)(3) and 501(c)(4) nonprofit organizations; 209 
(5) Investors and philanthropic organizations; 210 
(6) Research institutions; 211 
(7) Government agencies; and 212 
(8) Other stakeholders identified by the Deputy Mayor for Planning and 213 
Economic Development. 214 
(c) Innovation clusters established or supported pursuant to this section shall engage in 215 
one or more of the following activities: 216 
(1) Cross-sector collaboration; 217

10 
 
(2) Research and commercialization partnerships; 218 
(3) Workforce pipeline development; 219 
(4) Entrepreneurial support; 220 
(5) Investment attraction; 221 
(6) Shared problem-solving initiatives; 222 
(7) Industry convenings; or 223 
(8) Other activities to support the growth of the public-interest technology sector 224 
as identified by the Deputy Mayor for Planning and Economic Development. 225 
TITLE IV. TECH FOR GOOD MARKETING STRATEGY. 226 
Sec. 401. Short title. 227 
This title may be cited as the “Tech for Good Marketing Strategy Act of 2026”. 228 
Sec. 402. Tech for Good Working Group. 229 
(a) There is established a Tech for Good Working Group. 230 
(b) The Working Group shall be convened by the Deputy Mayor for Planning and 231 
Economic Development. 232 
(c) The Working Group shall include representatives from:  233 
(1) The Office of the Deputy Mayor for Planning and Economic Development; 234 
(2) The Office of the Deputy Mayor for Education; 235 
(3) The Department of Employment Services; 236 
(4) Institutions of higher education located in the District; 237 
(5) Qualified public-interest technology companies as defined in D.C. Official 238 
Code § 47-1818.21(6); 239 
(6) Workforce development organizations; 240

11 
 
(7) Nonprofit organizations focused on innovation or economic mobility; 241 
(8) The investment or entrepreneurial community; and 242 
(9) Other stakeholders identified by the Deputy Mayor for Planning and 243 
Economic Development. 244 
(d) The Working Group shall:  245 
(1) Develop recommendations for a District-wide “Tech for Good” marketing and 246 
branding strategy; 247 
(2) Identify opportunities to attract qualified public-interest technology companies 248 
to the District as defined in D.C. Official Code § 47-1818.21(6); 249 
(3) Identify strategies to strengthen workforce pipelines into innovation-focused 250 
industries for District residents; 251 
(4) Recommend opportunities for cross-sector collaboration and ecosystem 252 
development; 253 
(5) Identify barriers to growth within public-interest technology sectors; and 254 
(6) Recommend additional policies or investments as identified by the Deputy 255 
Mayor for Planning and Economic Development. 256 
(e) Within one year after the effective date of this title, the Working Group shall submit a 257 
report to the Council summarizing its findings and recommendations. 258

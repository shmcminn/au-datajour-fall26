To amend the District of Columbia Traffic Adjudication Act of 1978 to remove the penalty equal 7 
to the amount of a civil fine for failure to answer a notice of infraction within 30 days when 8 
the infraction has been detected by an automated traffic enforcement system or involves a 9 
parking, standing, stopping or pedestrian offense. 10 
 11 
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 12 
act may be cited as the “Parking and Traffic Ticket Penalty Reduction Amendment Act of 2026”. 13

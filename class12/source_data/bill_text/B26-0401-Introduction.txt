 To amend Chapter 23 of Title 16 of the D.C. Official Code to clarify juvenile case and social 13 
records access provided to appropriate Council entities and the Office of the 14 
Ombudsperson for Children for the purposes of enhancing oversight of the child welfare 15 
and juvenile justice systems. 16 
 17 
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 18 
Act may be cited as the "Youth Records Transparency and Oversight Amendment Act of 2025”. 19 
Sec. 2. Chapter 23 of Title 16 of the D.C. Official Code as follows: 20 
(a) Section 2331(c)(4) is amended by adding new subsubsections (H-J) to read as 21 
follows: 22 
“(H) In delinquency or need of supervision cases, the Chair of the Council Committee 23 
with oversight over the Department of Youth Rehabilitation Services and their designee(s) when 24 
necessary for the discharge of their official duties; 25 
“(I) In neglect cases, the Chair of the Committee with oversight over the Child and 26 
Family Services Agency and their designee(s) when necessary for the discharge of their official 27 
duties; and  28 
“(J) The Office of the Ombudsperson for Children when necessary for the discharge of its 29 
official duties.”. 30

To amend The Grandparent Caregivers Pilot Programs Establishment Act of 2005 to increase the 24 
rate of subsidy provided to grandparent and closes relative caregivers, to annually adjust 25 
the subsidy for inflation, and to allow for the grandparent caregiver subsidy to be granted 26 
until the child reaches age 21; To amend The Close Relative Caregiver Subsidy Pilot 27 
Program Establishment Amendment Act of 2019 to allow for the close relative caregiver 28 
subsidy to be granted until the child reaches age 21. 29 
 30 
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 31 
act may be cited as the “Increasing Support for Grandparent and Close Relative Caregivers 32 
Amendment Act of 2025”. 33 
 Sec. 2. The Grandparent Caregivers Pilot Programs Establishment Act of 2005, effective 34 
March 8, 2006 (D.C. Law 16-69; D.C. Official Code § 4-251.01 et seq.) is amended as follows: 35

2 
 
 (a) Section 103(d) (D.C. Official Code § 4-251.03(d)) is amended by striking the phrase 36 
“may continue until the child reaches 18 years of age” and inserting the phrase “may continue 37 
until the child reaches 21 years of age” in its place. 38 
(b) Section 104(b) (D.C. Official Code § 4-251.04(b)) is amended by striking the phrase 39 
“the Mayor shall establish by rule the amount of a subsidy a grandparent or relative caregiver is 40 
eligible to receive under this subchapter, provided, that the subsidy shall be at least 66%, but no 41 
more than 105%,” and inserting the phrase “the Mayor shall establish by rule  the amount of a 42 
subsidy a grandparent or relative caregiver is eligible to receive under this subchapter, provided 43 
that the subsidy shall be no less than the amount of $27.92 in Fiscal Year 2027 and adjusted for 44 
inflation annually thereafter.” 45 
Sec. 3. Section 103 of The Close Relative Caregiver Subsidy Pilot Program 46

(a) Subsection (e) (D.C. Official Code §4–251.23) is amended by striking the phrase 49 
“may continue until the child reaches 18 years of age” and inserting the phrase “may continue 50 
until the child reaches 21 years of age” in its place. 51

To amend the Prevention of Child Abuse and Neglect Act of 1977 to require the Child and 21 
Family Services Agency to inform, at the initiation of an investigation, caretakers of any 22 
legal resources available to the caretaker, the availability of the Office of the 23 
Ombudsperson for Children as a resource for complaints, and their right to counsel 24 
during critical stages of neglect proceedings. 25 
 26 
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this act may 27 
be cited as the “Empowering Parents in CFSA Investigations Amendment Act of 2025”. 28 
 Sec. 2. Title 1 of the Prevention of Child Abuse and Neglect Act of 1977, effective 29 
September 23, 1977 (D.C. Law 2-22; D.C. Official Code § 4-1301.04 et seq.), is amended as 30 
follows: 31 
(a) A new section (c)(3) is added to read as follows: 32 
 “(3) Include written notification of legal resources available to the child’s 33 
reported caretaker or caretakers during the investigation process. 34 
(b) Section 3(B) is amended to read as follows: 35

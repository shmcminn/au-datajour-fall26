To amend Chapter 46 of Title 47 of the District of Columbia Official Code to provide a limited 18 
real property tax abatement for the real property located at 4020 Minnesota Avenue, N.E., 19 
Washington, D.C. 20019, known for tax and assessment purposes as Lot 811 in Square 20 
5052, conditioned upon the owner making qualifying capital improvements to the 21 
property in an amount at least equal to the value of the tax abatement received, 22 
maintaining the affordability of the residential units, complying with District law, and 23 
satisfying reporting and certification requirements; and to authorize the Mayor to 24 
suspend, terminate, or recapture the tax abatement for failure to satisfy the requirements 25 
of this act. 26 
 27 
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 28 
act may be cited as the “Park 7 Affordable Housing Preservation Tax Abatement Amendment Act 29 
of 2026”. 30

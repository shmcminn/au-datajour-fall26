A BILL 
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA 
To amend the Legalization of Marijuana for Medical Treatment Initiative of 1999 to create a new 
medical cannabis retailer craft preparation endorsement, and to establish specific 
requirements that must be satisfied for the Alcoholic Beverage and Cannabis Board to 
approve and issue a craft preparation endorsement. 
BE IT ENACTED BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this 
act may be cited as the "Medical Cannabis Retailer Craft Preparation Endorsement Amendment 
Act of 2025". 
Sec. 2. The Legalization of Marijuana for Medical Treatment Initiative of 1999, effective 
February 25, 2010 (D.C. Law 13 -315; D.C. Official Code§ 7-1671.01 et seq.), is amended as 
follows: 
(a) Section 7 (D.C. Official Code § 7-1671.01) is amended by adding a new paragraph 
(15B) to read as follows : 
"(15B) "Preroll" means a cigarette containing raw or dried cannabis flower or 
other material from the cannabis plant wrapped in rolling paper or other material. 
(b) A new section 7g is added to read as follows: 
"Sec. 7g. Craft preparation endorsement. 
"(a) A holder of a retailer license shall be eligible to apply to the ABC Board for a craft 
preparation endorsement.

“(b) A holder of a craft preparation endorsement may prepare the following types of 34 
medical cannabis products at their licensed premises for sale to qualifying patients and 35 
caregivers: 36 
 “(1) Baked goods, beverages, chocolates, confections, and other edibles, provided 37

 “(2) Lotions, soaps, ointments, and other products intended to be applied to the 40

 “(3) Prerolls. 42 
“(c)(1) Medical cannabis products prepared under a craft preparation endorsement shall 43 
be prepared, tested, packaged, and labeled in accordance with the requirements of this act and 44 
rules issued by the Mayor. 45 
“(2) All medical cannabis product prepared under a craft preparation endorsement 46 
shall be shelf-stable and shall not require refrigeration to avoid spoilation.  47 
“(3) A holder of a craft preparation endorsement shall not prepare or manufacture 48 
transdermal patches. 49 
“(d)(1) Medical cannabis products prepared under a craft preparation endorsement may 50 
be sold only to qualifying patients and their caregivers.  51 
“(2) A holder of a craft preparation endorsement that also holds a delivery 52 
endorsement may deliver medical cannabis products prepared under the endorsement to 53 
qualifying patients and their caregivers. 54 
 “(3) A holder of a craft preparation endorsement shall not distribute or sell 55 
medical cannabis products to retailers, internet retailers, manufacturers, or unlicensed 56

 “(e) A holder of a craft preparation endorsement may devote no more than 20% of the 59

 “(f)(1) A holder of a craft preparation endorsement may store no more than 2 kilograms 61

 “(c)(1) The BID taxes for taxable properties in the BID shall be as follows: 57 
  “(A) The amount of $0.14 per $100 in assessed value for Class 2 Properties, 58 
excluding hotels;  59 
  “(B) The amount of $120 per hotel or motel room annually; 60 
  “(C) The amount of $110 per unit for Class 1A or 1B Properties; 61 
  “(D) The amount of $10 per unit restricted to residents based upon income 62 
pursuant to a federal or District affordable housing program, capped at $1,500 per building; 63 
  “(E) The amount of $0.14 per $100 in assessed value for Class 3 Properties; 64 
  “(F) The amount of $0.14 per $100 in assessed value for surface parking lots; 65 
  “(G) The amount of $100 per building or portion of a building used primarily for 66 
religious worship or religious services, including a church, synagogue, mosque, temple, or 67
